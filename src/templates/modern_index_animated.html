🌾 Welcome to Smart Agriculture Analytics!

This platform helps farmers make data-driven decisions using AI and machine learning. Let's walk through the key features that will help optimize your farming operations.

📊 Dashboard - Your Farm Overview

The Dashboard shows real-time metrics about your farm including crop health, weather conditions, and performance indicators. It's your central command center for monitoring everything at a glance.

🔬 Comprehensive Analysis

Enter your soil conditions, weather data, and crop information to get AI-powered recommendations for crop selection, yield predictions, fertilizer suggestions, and irrigation scheduling.

🐛 Pest Detection System

Input environmental conditions to get early warnings about potential pest threats. The system analyzes temperature, humidity, and seasonal patterns to predict pest risks before they become problems.

💰 Market Intelligence

Get price forecasts and market trend analysis to help you decide the best time to sell your crops. Plan your harvesting and marketing strategy based on predicted market conditions.

🚀 Ready to Start?

You're all set! Start with the Comprehensive Analysis to get recommendations for your current growing conditions. Look for the help tooltips (?) throughout the site for additional guidance.
